## Accepted Papers

We are pleased to announce the papers accepted to EARL 2026. All accepted papers will be presented orally at the workshop on 8 November 2026 (times are local Rome time, CET). See the [full schedule](https://earl-workshop.github.io/#schedule) for the complete programme.

### Long Talks

- **Beyond Posts: Recommending Conversations in Threads Feed** — _Xiuyan Ni, Qing Feng, Eric He, Akhil Nadigatla, Adam Kozdrowicz, Emerson Wenzel, Runbo Jiang, Nicholas Joodi, Xiaoyu Cao, Zichao Li, Joy Qiu, Shuting Wang, Shujian Bu and Ivan Ji_ (09:40–10:00).
- **Dialogue Generation for Conversational Recommendation Using User Search Interactions and Knowledge Graphs** — _Donggyu Yoon, Minsung Hwang, Kwanghee Jang, Gowoon Hwang and Joyce Jiyoung Whang_ (11:30–11:50).
- **Format Compliance without Detectable Preference Gain: A Paired Audit of Post-Trained LLM Recommenders** — _Shiming Xie_ (11:50–12:10).

### Short Talks

- **From Raw IDs to Semantic Planning: How Recommender Systems Utilize Information at Scale** — _Changhong Jin, Shiqiu Yang, Roger Zhe Li, Yingjie Niu, Aghiles Salah, Mete Sertkan, Zheng Ju, Xingsheng Guo, Huifeng Guo, Ruihai Dong and Barry Smyth_ (10:00–10:10).
- **Encoded but Exposed: Language Embeddings can Reveal More Than They Should in Recommender Systems** — _Linda Beková and Rodrigo Alves_ (10:10–10:20).
- **When to Ask: A Framework for Interruption-Aware Conversational Recommendation** — _Sanjana Arun_ (10:20–10:30).
- **Validity Lag: Why LLM Recommender Evaluation Keeps Failing the Same Way** — _Shiming Xie_ (10:30–10:40).
- **Persona Aware Session-Based Recommendation with LLM-Enhanced Knowledge Graphs** — _Muskan Gupta, Priyanka Gupta and Jyotsana Khatri_ (12:10–12:20).
- **From a Large-Scale Teacher to Efficient Multimodal Students for Search Relevance Prediction** — _Tomasz Gniazdowski, Mohammadreza Momenifar, Nadia Vase, Shahram Khadivi, Wiktor Walentynowicz, Nicola Ueffing, Jan Pawłowski, Piotr Szymański and Abraham Bagherjeiran_ (12:20–12:30).
- **Preprocessing Choices in Fine-Tuning Data Matter: Utility and Demographic Sensitivity in LLM-Based Recommendation** — _Furkan Yasir Goksu, Yaren Yilmaz and Şule Gündüz Öğüdücü_ (12:30–12:40).
- **Revisiting Generative vs. Dense Retrieval: A Static Value Prior Reverses the Gap** — _Renbo Tu, Yang Liu and Shuying Sun_ (12:40–12:50).
