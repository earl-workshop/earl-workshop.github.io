## Accepted Papers

We are pleased to announce the papers accepted to EARL 2026. The assignment to oral and poster presentations will be announced with the detailed programme.

- **Beyond Posts: Recommending Conversations in Threads Feed** — _Xiuyan Ni, Qing Feng, Eric He, Akhil Nadigatla, Adam Kozdrowicz, Emerson Wenzel, Runbo Jiang, Nicholas Joodi, Xiaoyu Cao, Zichao Li, Joy Qiu, Shuting Wang, Shujian Bu and Ivan Ji_.
- **From Raw IDs to Semantic Planning: How Recommender Systems Utilize Information at Scale** — _Changhong Jin, Shiqiu Yang, Roger Zhe Li, Yingjie Niu, Aghiles Salah, Mete Sertkan, Zheng Ju, Xingsheng Guo, Huifeng Guo, Ruihai Dong and Barry Smyth_.
- **Dialogue Generation for Conversational Recommendation Using User Search Interactions and Knowledge Graphs** — _Donggyu Yoon, Minsung Hwang, Kwanghee Jang, Gowoon Hwang and Joyce Jiyoung Whang_.
- **From a Large-Scale Teacher to Efficient Multimodal Students for Search Relevance Prediction** — _Tomasz Gniazdowski, Mohammadreza Momenifar, Nadia Vase, Shahram Khadivi, Wiktor Walentynowicz, Nicola Ueffing, Jan Pawłowski, Piotr Szymański and Abraham Bagherjeiran_.
- **Encoded but Exposed: Language Embeddings can Reveal More Than They Should in Recommender Systems** — _Linda Beková and Rodrigo Alves_.
- **Preprocessing Choices in Fine-Tuning Data Matter: Utility and Demographic Sensitivity in LLM-Based Recommendation** — _Furkan Yasir Goksu, Yaren Yilmaz and Şule Gündüz Öğüdücü_.
- **Revisiting Generative vs. Dense Retrieval: A Static Value Prior Reverses the Gap** — _Renbo Tu, Yang Liu and Shuying Sun_.
- **Format Compliance without Detectable Preference Gain: A Paired Audit of Post-Trained LLM Recommenders** — _Shiming Xie_.
- **When to Ask: A Framework for Interruption-Aware Conversational Recommendation** — _Sanjana Arun_.
- **Persona Aware Session-Based Recommendation with LLM-Enhanced Knowledge Graphs** — _Muskan Gupta, Priyanka Gupta and Jyotsana Khatri_.
- **Validity Lag: Why LLM Recommender Evaluation Keeps Failing the Same Way** — _Shiming Xie_.
