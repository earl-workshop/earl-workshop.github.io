## Workshop format

EARL 2026 will be a half-day workshop held in person at CIKM 2026.

## Programme highlights

- **Invited talk:** Bum Jun Kim (The University of Tokyo) will give an invited talk on gradient vulnerability and its implications for LLM-based recommender systems ([details below](#invited-talk)).

- **Best Paper Award:** We will continue the award introduced in 2025 to recognize originality, technical excellence, and impact.
- **Best Paper Runner-Up recognitions:** Two runner-up recognitions will acknowledge additional outstanding contributions.

## Invited Talk

### Gradient Vulnerability and Its Implications for LLM-Based Recommender Systems

**Speaker:** Bum Jun Kim (The University of Tokyo)  
**Time:** 8 November 2026, 09:10–09:40 (local Rome time, CET) · 25 min talk + 5 min Q&A

**Abstract.** Gradients are widely used not only for training AI models but also for measuring importance and guiding decisions. In this talk, I will introduce a vulnerability in gradient measurement: even when two models implement the same function, their gradients can differ. The gradient is not uniquely determined within a specific model, and rather, multiple gradients can be measured in a Transformer. The problem will be demonstrated with empirical and theoretical evidence on LLMs, especially how gradient-based decisions can become unreliable in Transformers. I will deliver how to ensure reliable gradients to develop more trustworthy gradient-based decision-making methods. I will then discuss its implication and challenges in LLM-based recommender systems with several examples.
