## Workshop format

EARL 2026 will be a half-day workshop held in person at CIKM 2026.

## Programme highlights

- **Invited talks:** We will feature one or two invited talks from leading researchers and practitioners working on LLM-enhanced recommender systems.
- **Oral presentations:** All accepted papers will be presented orally, as long talks (15 min + 5 min Q&A) or short talks (8 min + 2 min Q&A).
- **Best Paper Award:** We will continue the award introduced in 2025 to recognize originality, technical excellence, and impact.
- **Best Paper Runner-Up recognitions:** Two runner-up recognitions will acknowledge additional outstanding contributions.
